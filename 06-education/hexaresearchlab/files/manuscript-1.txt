Neural Approaches to Citation Prediction
Alice Rahman, Dhaka Institute of Technology

Abstract: We study graph neural networks for predicting citation counts of newly
submitted manuscripts, using the submission graph of a mid-size journal.

1. Introduction
Citation prediction helps editors triage submissions. We model each manuscript
as a node in a heterogeneous graph of authors, references and venues.

2. Method
A two-layer graph attention network encodes the citation graph; a regression
head predicts the log citation count after 24 months.

3. Dataset
12,400 manuscripts submitted between 2018 and 2023, split by year. Code and the
anonymised split definitions are available from the corresponding author.
