Quantum Error Correction Benchmarks on Noisy Hardware
Bob Carter, Northgate University

Abstract: A benchmark suite for surface-code decoders running on current noisy
quantum devices, with a reproducible noise model.

1. Introduction
Decoder latency dominates logical error rates on today's devices. We benchmark
six decoders across three hardware backends.

2. Noise model
We adopt a depolarising model calibrated per backend, described in section 4.
