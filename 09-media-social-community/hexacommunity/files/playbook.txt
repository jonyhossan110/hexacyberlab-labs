Welcome to HexaCommunity!
HexaCommunity is a friendly membership platform: find members in the directory, join
groups, RSVP to events, download resources from the library and talk in discussions.

This getting-started playbook is FREE for every member tier.
- Create your profile and say hello in the Open Lounge group.
- RSVP early: meetup seats are limited.
- Be kind: moderators keep the space safe for everyone.
