by Marcus Webb

Chapter One

The ferry brought the storm in on a Tuesday, along with two
rival chefs, one crate of lemons, and absolutely no way off
the island until spring.

Ana Salt — she had heard every joke — set her knives on the
counter of the only kitchen that would have her. Across the
pass, Cinder Ashworth smiled the smile of a man who has never
once been rained on.

"One kitchen," he said. "One season."

"One winner," she said.
