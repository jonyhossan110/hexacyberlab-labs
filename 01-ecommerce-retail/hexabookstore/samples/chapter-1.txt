Chapter One

The library at Marrow Lane kept its own hours. It opened when it
pleased, closed when it pleased, and — on the last night of every
month — it rewrote itself.

Tomas the watchmaker noticed first. A book he had shelved under
HISTORY on Tuesday was, by Friday, a cookbook, bound in the same
green cloth, with his own handwriting in the margin of a recipe
for preserved lemons.

He did what any sensible craftsman would do. He locked the door,
made tea, and began, gear by gear, to take the building apart
in his mind.
