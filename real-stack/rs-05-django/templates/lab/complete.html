Business impact — what HexaClinic was risking

A clinic portal holds the most sensitive category of personal data there is. This one let any patient read every other patient's record by changing a number, let stored markup run in a doctor's browser, kept every password as a fast MD5 hash, and guarded the whole database with an admin password from the install runbook. In business language: a reportable health-data breach, regulator sanctions, and the end of the clinic's reputation — caused not by Django failing, but by a team switching off, one by one, the protections they chose it for.
