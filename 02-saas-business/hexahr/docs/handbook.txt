HexaHR — Employee Handbook (excerpt)
====================================

Welcome to the company. This handbook lives in the HexaHR documents area.

1. Working hours: 09:00–17:30, Monday to Friday.
2. Leave: request annual leave in HexaHR at least two weeks ahead.
   Your manager approves requests for their own team only.
3. Payroll: payslips are published on the last working day of the month.
   Your payslip is private — you can view only your own.
4. Personal data: your record contains your phone, home address and
   date of birth. Only you, your manager and HR may see it.
5. Passwords: change the password IT issues you on your first day.
   Never share your login.

— Human Resources
