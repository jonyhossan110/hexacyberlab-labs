HexaClaim Insurance — Policy Terms Summary (public)
---------------------------------------------------
1. Coverage applies only to the policyholder named on the policy.
2. Approved claims are paid once, up to the approved payout amount.
3. Payouts never exceed the amount claimed or the policy coverage limit.
4. Internal adjuster notes are confidential and never shown to customers.
5. Evidence files may be reviewed only by the claim owner and staff.

(This public summary is intentionally inconsistent with the training
build of this application — finding those gaps is the exercise.)
