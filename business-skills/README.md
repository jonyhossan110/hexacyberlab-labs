# Business Skills — The Career Ladder

You finished a lab category. You can find vulnerabilities. That is roughly half of being a penetration tester.

The other half is business: knowing what you are selling, putting it in writing, pricing it without panicking, talking to clients like a professional, running the engagement cleanly, writing a report people act on, and getting paid — every time. Nobody hires "the person who found the most XSS." They hire the person they trust with their production systems and their budget.

This module is that second half.

## How the ladder works

The lab programme has 11 categories. Each category you complete at 100% unlocks one business lesson here — in order. Finish a technical category, open the business door next to it. The two tracks climb together:

- **Technical track** — you learn to hack the application class you just studied.
- **Business track** — you learn the skill you would need to sell that exact work to a real client.

By the time you finish category 11, you will not just know how to test eleven kinds of applications. You will know how to scope the work, price it, get it authorized, run it, report it, get paid for it, and turn it into recurring revenue and a reputation.

## How to use these lessons

- **Read in order the first time.** Each lesson assumes the vocabulary of the ones before it.
- **They stand alone afterwards.** Every lesson is written so you can return to just that one — the scope template before a kickoff, the pricing lesson before a quote, the report lesson the night before delivery.
- **Do the checklists.** Each lesson ends with (or contains) a checklist or template. The templates are yours to copy and adapt. A checklist you actually use is worth more than ten chapters you only read.
- **Nothing here is legal advice.** Contracts, authorization, and payment law differ by country. The lessons teach you what must exist and why; for your first real engagement, have a local lawyer review your documents once. It is cheaper than learning the hard way.

## One rule that runs through all 11 lessons

**Never test anything you do not have written permission to test.** Every business skill in this module exists to make you a professional — and that rule is the line between a professional and a criminal. Lesson 05 covers it in depth, but it applies from lesson 01 onward, and it applies to every lab outside this sandbox too.
